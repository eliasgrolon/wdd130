Splash Water White Rafting completely changed our perspective on adventure! White water rafting exceeded all our expectations! The professional guide made us feel safe every second, combining pure adrenaline with unforgettable natural scenery. Without a doubt, an experience we will repeat every year.

History

Founded in 2012 by a passionate group of river rescue professionals and extreme sports enthusiasts, Splash Water White Rafting was born with the firm purpose of bringing the excitement of wild rivers to everyone. We started with a single inflatable raft and a shared dream in Patagonia; today, we operate world-class expeditions on multiple rivers, always keeping the safety of our passengers and absolute respect for the environment as our highest priority.
