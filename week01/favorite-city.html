Buenos Aires, Argentina

Buenos Aires , historically called Santa María del Buen Aire , is the capital and most populous city of Argentina . Its official names are Ciudad de Buenos Aires or Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires ( CABA ). It is located in the east-central region of the country, on the southern bank of the Río de la Plata , in the Pampas region . It is one of the 24 districts, or "first-order jurisdictions," that make up the country.

Buenos Aires is a cosmopolitan city and a major tourist destination. Its complex infrastructure makes it one of the most important metropolises in the Americas and a global city of "alpha-" status, given its influence in commerce, finance, fashion, art, gastronomy, education, entertainment, and especially its distinctive culture. Its per capita income is among the highest in the region. In 2022, it was the most visited city in Latin America. It is considered one of the twenty-five most influential cities in the world.
